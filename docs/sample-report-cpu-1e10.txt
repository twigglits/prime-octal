5. WHY OCTAL=HEX AT THE LAST DIGIT: admissible density phi(b)/b
   a base's last digit only screens the primes that divide the base.
   base b   factorization      phi(b)/b     primes screened by last digit
   2        2                   50.00%      2
   8        2^3                 50.00%      2  (only!)
   16       2^4                 50.00%      2  (only!)
   32       2^5                 50.00%      2  (only!)
   10       2*5                 40.00%      2, 5
   6        2*3                 33.33%      2, 3
   30       2*3*5               26.67%      2, 3, 5
   210      2*3*5*7             22.86%      2, 3, 5, 7
   -> every power of two sticks at 50%: it only ever screens the prime 2.
      octal and hex are informationally identical at the last digit.
      the real corridors tighten with PRIMORIAL bases (6,30,210,...),
      which is exactly what the base-8 *multi-digit* predictor reconstructs.
