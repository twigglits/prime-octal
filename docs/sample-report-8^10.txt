9. THE BASE-8 IDENTITIES BEHIND THE PREDICTOR
   8 ≡ 1 (mod 7): n ≡ its octal digit sum (mod 7)
   8 ≡ -1 (mod 3): n ≡ its alternating octal digit sum (mod 3)
   8^i mod 5 cycles 1,3,4,2: weighted octal digit sum gives n mod 5
   last octal digit carries n mod 8; odd squares all end in octal 1
